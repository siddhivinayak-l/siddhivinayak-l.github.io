About

Where AI meets ions, interfaces and devices.

I work on electrochemical systems that change over time. Ionic motion, surface interactions and device history can create useful behaviour — if they are measured carefully and understood physically.

Research

Understand the system. Then make it useful.

My approach combines direct device measurements, electrochemical analysis, automation and machine learning. The purpose is to expose mechanisms, improve measurement and develop better ways to use physical response.

Ionic transport under confinement

Ion motion, surface interactions and history-dependent behaviour in nanofluidic and microfluidic solution systems.

Memory and adaptive devices

Ionic and electrochemical devices for programmable conductance, retention and neuromorphic response.

AI-assisted device analysis

Experimentally grounded workflows that connect measured data with physical meaning and more efficient studies.
