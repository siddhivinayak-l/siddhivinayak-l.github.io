About

Iontronic devices & electrochemical data.

My doctoral research focuses on ionic memory in nanofluidic 2D nanochannels for brain-inspired computing. My machine-learning work uses electrochemical data from perovskites and batteries. I am interested in AI-guided experiments that inform material parameters, experimental recipes and device design.

Research

From measurement to memory and prediction.

I combine device experiments, electrochemical analysis and machine learning to understand physical behaviour and identify useful design choices.

Ion transport & memory

Confined ion motion, interfacial processes and device history. Linking these mechanisms to retention, programmable conductance and synaptic response.

Electrochemical data & prediction

Machine learning for perovskite and battery data, including material parameters, optical response and impedance.

Automated experimentation

Instrument control, programmed voltage pulses and reproducible analysis. A foundation for AI-guided experiments.
